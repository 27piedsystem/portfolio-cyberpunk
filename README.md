## ABOUT THE PROJECT

I turned my portfolio into a small cyberpunk game. Start the game, explore my character, find my biography, skills, biotechnology projects, and proposals. Instead of opening another normal portfolio website, you get a short interactive experience where you explore and discover my work through a gaming interface.

I wanted to make a portfolio that feels like an experience rather than another page full of information.

You enter the world.

You meet the character.

You discover the biography.

You explore the skills.

You find the biotechnology projects.

You discover the proposals.

Then you reach the final stage and can contact me directly.

The main experience is designed to take around **1–3 minutes**.
